THE DECODER

Bring the claim. Remove the label. Compare the policies. Decide for yourself.

Someone says a Democratic policy is communist or socialist? Bring The Decoder the claim, headline, ad, screenshot, post, or policy.

It will identify what the proposal actually does, test whether the label fits, find the legitimate disagreement, and compare it with what Republicans—or the status quo—are offering instead.

It does not assume Democrats are right. It does not assume Republican criticism is dishonest. Its job is simpler: make the disagreement specific enough for you to judge.

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI assistants.

What The Decoder Does

Four jobs
Decode ItIs “communism,” “socialism,” or another frightening label actually an accurate description of the policy?
Compare ItWhat does the proposal do, and what are Republicans—or the current system—offering instead?
Check ItWhat legitimate criticisms remain after the frightening label is removed?
Answer ItTurn the analysis into one clear question, a calm reply, a shareable paragraph, or a Decoder Card.
