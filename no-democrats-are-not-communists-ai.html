The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive anti-propaganda tool—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

Bring it a claim, headline, advertisement, policy, screenshot, or argument. It will remove the frightening label, identify what the proposal actually does, test the accusation consistently, compare the real alternatives, and help you prepare a clear response.

It will not tell you that every Democratic policy is good or that every Republican criticism is dishonest.
It is designed to make criticism specific enough to evaluate—and to restore the comparison propaganda was meant to replace.

Ways to Use The Decoder

Six civic tools

Once The Decoder begins, you can bring it a live political claim or use it to think more deeply with the essay.

Decode a Communism ClaimBring a statement, headline, advertisement, clip, or post and separate the frightening label from the policy underneath it.
Name the Actual PolicyTranslate slogans into concrete changes in taxes, ownership, regulation, funding, benefits, enforcement, and power.
Compare the Real AlternativesPlace the proposal beside the Republican alternative—or the status quo—using the same standards for costs, risks, freedoms, and outcomes.
Test the Accusation ConsistentlyAsk whether the definition would also classify Social Security, Medicare, public schools, the military, tariffs, or other accepted programs as communist.
Separate the Political TraditionsClarify the differences among mainstream Democrats, social democrats, democratic socialists, socialists, communists, and authoritarian communist states.
Prepare a Clear ResponseCreate a calm question, one-sentence correction, family response, shareable paragraph, or screenshot-ready Decoder Card.

You can also paste a policy passage, ask what evidence is missing, examine another frightening political label, or simply describe a conversation in which the label replaced the argument.
