THE DECODER

Bring the claim. Remove the label. Compare the policies. Decide for yourself.

Someone says a Democratic policy is communist or socialist? Bring The Decoder the claim, headline, ad, screenshot, post, or policy.

It identifies what the proposal actually does, tests whether the label fits, finds the legitimate disagreement, and compares it with the actual alternative.

It does not assume Democrats are right. It does not assume Republican criticism is dishonest. Its job is simpler: make the disagreement specific enough for you to judge.

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI assistants.

Image Cards Should Look Like This

Publication, not campaign ad

Intelligence briefing × newspaper fact-check × civic document × Earth Drama. Warm ivory/newsprint, near-black type, restrained gold, optional clarification blue, and red only when it carries actual semantic meaning. No clutter, fake 3D, oversized shaded boxes, or generic political-infographic styling.

Built-in guardrail: The image generator is instructed to preserve the exact locked card copy. It may adjust typography and spacing, but not silently rewrite, truncate, omit, or invent facts.
