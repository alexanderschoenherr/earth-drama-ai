An Earth Drama AI Experience

Continue The Best Politician Is the Worst Politician with AI

The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you separate a system’s machinery from its mission, trace what incentives actually select for, distinguish authenticity from truth, examine what a winner reveals about the prizes, and carry the question “optimize what?” into AI, institutions, and your own life.

It will not tell you which politician, institution, technology, or life strategy to support.
It is designed to help you distinguish winning from serving, measurement from meaning, and greater capability from wiser purpose.

Ways to Explore the Essay

Once the Companion begins, you can take the conversation in any of these directions.

Separate Mechanism from MissionIdentify what a system is for, what machinery it uses, what became measurable, and whether the machinery is still serving the end.
Follow the IncentiveTrace what an institution says it values, what it actually rewards, how people adapt, and what kind of behavior the system selects.
Authenticity, Truth, and ShamelessnessExamine the difference between being genuinely oneself, being factually truthful, and converting brazenness or exposure into advantage.
Examine the Winner, Examine the PrizeAsk what an environment had to reward for a particular person, organization, strategy, or outcome to become exceptionally fit.
Optimize What?Apply the essay to AI, algorithms, business, technology, or any system becoming more capable at pursuing an objective.
Bring the Means–Mission Reversal HomeFind where money, productivity, attention, identity, status, credentials, or a practice may have started competing with the reason it mattered.

You can also paste a passage, challenge the thesis, bring in another institution or political figure, ask about AI, connect the essay to another framework, or simply describe something that feels like “the machinery works, but something is wrong.”
