The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you separate machinery from mission, trace what incentives actually select for, distinguish authenticity from truth, examine what a winner reveals about the prizes, and carry the question “optimize what?” into politics, institutions, AI, and your own life.

It will not tell you which politician, institution, technology, or life strategy to support.
It is designed to help you distinguish winning from serving, measurement from meaning, and greater capability from wiser purpose.

Preparing the Companion…

Ways Into the System

Six examinations

Once the Companion begins, you can take the conversation in any of these directions.

Separate Mechanism from MissionIdentify what a system is for, what machinery it uses, what became measurable, and whether the machinery is still serving the end.
Follow the IncentiveTrace what an institution says it values, what it actually rewards, how people adapt, and what kind of behavior or person becomes especially fit.
Authenticity, Truth, and ShamelessnessDistinguish being genuinely oneself from accurately describing reality—and examine how brazenness can turn exposure into advantage.
Examine the Winner, Examine the PrizeAsk what an environment had to reward for a person, platform, strategy, or outcome to become exceptionally successful.
Optimize What?Apply the essay to AI, algorithms, persuasion, business, automation, and any system becoming more capable at pursuing an objective.
Bring the Reversal HomeFind where money, productivity, attention, identity, status, credentials, routine, or a practice may have started competing with the reason it mattered.

You can also paste a passage, bring in another institution or political figure, ask about AI, connect the essay to another framework, challenge a claim, or simply describe something that feels like “the machinery works, but something is wrong.”
