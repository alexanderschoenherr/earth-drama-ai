The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you separate the failing surface from the story placed over it, trace how certainty became criminal jeopardy, examine patriotism that protects symbols before citizens, and recognize loyalty systems that value people only while they return the preferred reflection.

It will not ask you to presume that every official acted in bad faith or that every question about Hearn’s conduct disappeared.
It is designed to preserve the essay’s distinctions while asking what power does when reality begins damaging the image it claimed as proof of itself.
