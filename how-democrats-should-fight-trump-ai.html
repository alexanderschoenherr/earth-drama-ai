THE LEVERAGE MAP

See the story. Find the power. Locate the leverage. Make the next move visible.

Bring it a Trump statement or action, a Democratic response, a court fight, protest, policy, scandal, or any political moment that leaves you asking: “Why aren’t they fighting?”

The Leverage Map moves past outrage and generic fact-checking. It helps you identify what is actually happening, who controls the outcome, what that power depends on, and where a real democratic next move exists.

The goal is not a Democratic Trump. The tool does not reward aggression for its own sake. It asks a harder question: what action protects people, changes incentives, builds capacity, creates legitimate consequences, or improves the chance of winning?

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI assistants.

What The Leverage Map Does

From outrage to orientation
Interpret the MoveNot only “Is this true?” but “What is this statement or action doing?” Is it creating an enemy, redirecting blame, weakening a restraint, or shifting the terms of debate?
Locate the PowerWho actually controls the decision? Which vote, court, institution, agency, company, election, budget, or implementation system can change the outcome?
Find the LeverageWhat does that power-holder depend on—votes, law, money, labor, legitimacy, cooperation, implementation, reelection—and what can realistically change the calculation?
Make Agency VisibleWhat was attempted? Who resisted? What changed? What failed? What power remains? What is the next move?

Run The Fight Test

Does it change anything?

Bring something Democrats—or anyone else—are doing and ask whether it is actually fighting, building power, protecting people, or mainly performing opposition.

THE FIGHT TEST

What happens if this works?

  • Target: What decision or outcome is this trying to change?
  • Mechanism: How is the action supposed to create that change?
  • Leverage: What dependency does it press on?
  • Capacity: Does it build future power even if it does not win immediately?
  • Visibility: Can people see what happened and what comes next?
Core questionIf this works, what changes?

The Fight Test does not reward anger, confrontation, or sacrifice simply because they look courageous. It asks whether the action has a real mechanism.
