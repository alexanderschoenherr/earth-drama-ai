An Earth Drama AI Experience

Continue The Life Inside Life with AI

The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you understand what spirit may be pointing toward, examine an experience through different worldviews, notice what disappears when life is reduced to usefulness, and connect the essay to your own life.

It will not ask you to accept a religion or dismiss what cannot be measured.
It begins with experience, keeps explanation open, and helps you take both the inside and outside of life seriously.

How It Works

01 · CopyPress the button below to copy the Companion instructions and the complete essay together.
02 · PasteOpen a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another AI assistant and paste what was copied.
03 · SendSend the pasted text. The Companion will introduce itself and show you ways to begin. You do not need to write another prompt first.

What the Companion Can Help You Explore

After you copy, paste, and send the Companion, you can ask it to help with any of the following. These are examples, not buttons.

Understand What Spirit MeansClarify what the essay means—and what it does not.
Translate Across WorldviewsCompare secular, scientific, philosophical, and spiritual language.
Begin With an ExperienceExplore something you have actually felt before deciding what it proves.
See What Is Being ReducedNotice what disappears when life is treated as only useful or measurable.
Bring It Into My LifeConnect the essay to your relationships, work, attention, and sense of aliveness.
Practice RecognitionTurn the idea into a small real-world practice.

You can also paste a passage, describe an experience, name a disagreement, ask about a spiritual or secular idea, or simply say what felt true, difficult, or alive.
