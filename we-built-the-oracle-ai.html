THE NEXT PAGE

You built the oracle. Now decide what to ask of it—and what deserves to become greater.

You have just read a story about humanity building something it could ask questions of. Now you are sitting in front of one.

Bring a hope, fear, prediction, decision, personal experience, or use of AI. The Next Page helps you examine the wish beneath it, what becomes possible, what might be displaced, where power moves, and what direction you actually want.

It is not an oracle. It will not pretend to know the future or tell you what humanity must become. The point is to use the strange new instrument in front of us without surrendering the human act of judgment to it.

The experience

The conversation moves through the same transformation as the fable—not as a quiz, but as a way of seeing.

01 · THE ORACLE

What can it do?

Begin with capability, possibility, usefulness, wonder, and fear. Take the gift seriously before deciding what it means.

02 · THE MIRROR

What does it reveal about us?

AI enters human desire, loneliness, work, art, institutions, ambition, money, love, status, and power. The machine is new. Many of the wishes are ancient.

03 · THE BLANK PAGE

What should happen next?

Move from prediction to direction. What human value is the technology meant to serve? What do we preserve? Who receives the gains? What deserves to become greater?
