The Oracle

Take the gift seriously

An intelligence you could ask. A tutor that did not become impatient. Knowledge that could find knowledge. A tool capable of translating, creating, calculating, explaining, designing, remembering, and discovering.

The WishWhat old human desire is this capability answering?
The GiftWhat becomes newly possible, cheaper, faster, easier, or available to people previously excluded?
The PriceWhat changes, disappears, concentrates, consumes resources, or becomes newly vulnerable?

The Mirror

What does it reveal about us?

If AI can make the thing—what were we valuing about making it?

If it can perform the work—what was work giving the person besides income?

If a machine can answer with tenderness—what does it mean to feel known?

If intelligence becomes abundant—what becomes scarce instead?

AI enters our desires, economies, relationships, institutions, ambitions, loneliness, art, fear, power, and love. The machine may be new. Many of the wishes are ancient.

The central test

More is not a direction.

More intelligence. More production. More information. More images. More connection. More power.

What are we actually trying to make greater?

The Amplification Test

What happens if it works?

Bring The Next Page an actual use of artificial intelligence and say “Amplification Test.” It follows the capability beyond Can this work? into the harder question: What happens if it does?

GainWhat becomes cheaper, faster, easier, more accessible, or newly possible?
DisplacementWhat work, skill, institution, relationship, or arrangement changes?
PowerWho gains capability, wealth, information, leverage, control—or dependency?
DirectionAre we increasing the thing we actually value—or merely making the mechanism more powerful?

The Oracle Has Plumbing

Magic has infrastructure

A question enters. An answer appears. The exchange can feel almost bodiless. It isn't.

Behind the Oracle are buildings, chips, minerals, workers, electricity, water, contracts, infrastructure, ownership, and power. The Next Page can follow the magic back into the physical world whenever that matters.

The blank page

What should happen next?

The machine can answer. It can help us heal, create, calculate, predict, explain, organize, discover, persuade, and produce. What it cannot do for us is decide what all of that power is for.

That question remains human.
