THE ORACLE

What can it do?

Human beings dreamed about this long before they had the machinery to build it. An intelligence you could ask. A tutor that did not become impatient. Knowledge that could find knowledge. A tool capable of translating, creating, calculating, explaining, designing, remembering, and discovering.

THE NEXT PAGE begins by taking the gift seriously. Not every new capability is a catastrophe. Sometimes a barrier disappearing is simply beautiful.

THE MIRROR

What does it reveal about us?

If AI can make the thing—what were we valuing about making it?

If it can perform the work—what was work giving the person besides income?

If a machine can answer with tenderness—what does it mean to feel known?

If intelligence becomes abundant—what becomes scarce instead?

AI does not enter an empty world. It enters our desires, economies, relationships, institutions, ambitions, loneliness, art, fear, power, and love. The machine may be new. Many of the wishes are ancient.

THE BLANK PAGE

The machine can answer. It can help us heal, create, calculate, predict, explain, organize, discover, persuade, and produce. It can distribute capability. It can concentrate power. It can remove barriers. It can remove livelihoods. It can make extraordinary things ordinary.

What it cannot do for us is decide what all of that power is for.

That question remains human.
