An Earth Drama AI Experience

Continue The Audit with AI

The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you trace how a real wound becomes a larger political warrant, audit a claim without dismissing the part that is true, test whether your own judgment can still move, and examine what happens when loyalty, power, or identity becomes protected from correction.

It will not tell you whether to support or oppose Trump.
It is designed to help you ask what evidence can count, where accountability becomes persecution—or persecution is used to discredit accountability—and what it means to remain correctable.

How It Works

01 · CopyPress “Copy Everything to Begin” to copy the Companion instructions and complete essay together.
02 · PasteOpen a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another AI assistant and paste what was copied.
03 · SendClick or tap the Send button—usually an arrow—or press Enter on a computer. You do not need to read, edit, or add anything first. The Companion will introduce itself and show you how to begin.

Ways to Explore the Essay

Once the Companion begins, you can take the conversation in any of these directions.

Trace the Grievance MachineFollow how a fact or wound becomes a grievance, story, worldview, warrant, and claim to power or exemption.
Audit a ClaimSeparate what happened from what it proves, what is inferred, and what evidence would count against the larger claim.
Test My JudgmentAsk whether a leader, movement, institution, or belief is still being judged—or has become protected from negative evidence.
Accountability or Persecution?Examine the difficult boundary between legitimate scrutiny, institutional abuse, reform, retaliation, and exemption.
Loyalty, Power, and the RepublicExplore republican versus royal logic, personal grievance versus public interest, and loyalty that follows judgment versus replaces it.
Bring the Audit HomeTurn the essay toward a hero, wound, identity, self-story, or belief you may not want reality to examine.

You can also paste a passage, challenge the essay, ask whether a factual claim is fair, bring in another leader or movement, describe a personal situation, or simply say where you felt resistance.

Privacy: This page does not access your AI account or send anything automatically. The button only copies text to your clipboard. After you paste it into an AI assistant, that service’s own privacy and memory settings apply. The Companion is instructed not to invent personal details, pressure political belief, or treat disagreement as a defect.
