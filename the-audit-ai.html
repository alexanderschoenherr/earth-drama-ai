Continue The Audit with AI

The Earth Drama Companion is a set of instructions you paste into ChatGPT or another AI assistant. Once sent, it turns this essay into an interactive conversation—no prompt-writing or AI experience required.

It can help you examine the one-page shield at the center of the story, trace how a real wound becomes a warrant and then a government, test whether judgment can still move, explore how a leader claims the right to translate every verdict, and turn the question of correctability back toward your own life.

It will not tell you whether to support or oppose Trump.
It is designed to help you distinguish justice from exemption, reform from revenge, a president from a king, and conviction from a belief that no outside reality is permitted to correct.

Ways to Explore the Essay

Six examinations

Once the Companion begins, you can take the conversation in any of these directions.

Read the One-Page ShieldExamine the government seal, the single signature, the former lawyer acting for the state, the proposed protections, and why Trump appears on both sides of the arrangement.
Trace the Wound into PowerFollow a real injury through grievance, story, worldview, warrant, retaliation, exemption, institutional capture, and expanded authority.
Audit a ClaimSeparate what happened from what it proves, what the true part legitimately supports, what was added, and what evidence could count against the conclusion.
Test Whether Judgment Can MoveAsk whether support remains a conclusion—or has become a premise that decides in advance what every new fact is allowed to mean.
Republic, Kingship, and the Citizen’s NoExplore interpretive sovereignty, the difference between occupying and absorbing an office, and why saying no to your own leader is a democratic act.
Bring the Audit HomeExamine a wound, hero, country, faith, relationship, identity, cause, or story that protects you—and may therefore be protected from reality.

You can also paste a passage, challenge the essay, ask whether a factual or legal claim is fair, bring in another leader or movement, describe a personal situation, or simply say where you felt resistance.
